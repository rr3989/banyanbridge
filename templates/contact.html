Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our foundation

How can I donate to Banyan Bridge Foundation?

You can donate through our website using various payment methods, including UPI, bank transfer, or credit/debit cards. All donations are tax-exempt under 80G.

What volunteer opportunities are available?

We offer various volunteer roles including teaching assistants, program coordinators, skills trainers, and outreach ambassadors. Opportunities are flexible and can be tailored to your skills and availability.

How are donations utilized?

Donations are used to support our educational programs, including learning materials, teacher training, infrastructure development, and operational costs. We maintain complete transparency in our financial reporting.

Can I visit your learning centers?

Yes, we welcome visitors to our learning centers. Please contact us in advance to schedule a visit and learn more about our programs firsthand.
